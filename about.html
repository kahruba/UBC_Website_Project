About The University of British Columbia

The University of British Columbia is one of Canada's leading public research universities, known for excellence in teaching, learning and research.

UBC provides a diverse and welcoming environment where students, faculty and researchers work together to create a positive impact on the world.

Who We Are

Founded in 1915, the University of British Columbia has grown into a globally recognized centre for education and research. With campuses in Vancouver and the Okanagan, UBC welcomes students from Canada and around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide outstanding education, encourage innovative research and inspire students to become responsible leaders who contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Vision

UBC strives to build a better future through knowledge, innovation and collaboration. We aim to create an inclusive community where new ideas are encouraged and people can achieve their full potential.

Why Choose UBC?

Academic Excellence

High-quality education across a wide range of disciplines, helping students achieve their academic goals.

Research & Innovation

UBC encourages discoveries and innovative ideas that can help address important global challenges.

Global Community

Students and researchers from diverse backgrounds come together to learn, collaborate and share ideas.

Student Experience

Students have opportunities to learn, grow and participate in a wide range of academic and campus activities.

Building A Better Future

At UBC, education goes far beyond the boundaries of the classroom. It is about developing knowledge, curiosity, creativity, and critical thinking while encouraging students to explore new ideas and perspectives. We believe that education should empower individuals to use what they learn to address real-world challenges, inspire positive change, and make meaningful contributions to their communities and the world. Through collaboration, innovation, and a commitment to lifelong learning, UBC strives to create an environment where students can grow not only as scholars, but also as responsible and engaged individuals who are prepared to make a lasting impact on society.
