Admissions at UBC

UBC welcomes students from Canada and around the world. Our admissions process is designed to help students find programs that match their interests, goals and academic background. Whether you are applying as an undergraduate or graduate student, you can explore a wide range of programs and prepare your application carefully before submitting it.

Choose Your Study Path

Undergraduate Admissions

Undergraduate programs at UBC provide students with a strong academic foundation and opportunities to explore different areas of study.

Students can choose from a wide range of faculties, programs and academic disciplines based on their interests and future goals.

Graduate Admissions

Graduate programs provide opportunities for advanced study, research and professional development across many academic disciplines.

Applicants can explore master's and doctoral programs and identify opportunities that match their academic interests and research goals.

Admission Requirements

Admission requirements can vary depending on the program and level of study. Applicants should carefully review the requirements for their chosen program.

Academic Qualifications

Applicants should meet the academic requirements associated with their selected program and level of study.

English Language

Applicants may need to demonstrate English language proficiency depending on their educational background and program requirements.

Supporting Documents

Applications may require academic records, personal information and other supporting documents depending on the selected program.

Program Requirements

Some programs may have additional requirements, prerequisites or application materials that applicants need to provide.

Application Process

Follow these general steps to prepare and submit your application to UBC.

1

Explore Programs

Research available programs and choose an area of study that matches your interests and goals.

2

Check Requirements

Review the academic and program-specific requirements before beginning your application.

3

Prepare Documents

Gather the required academic records and other supporting documents for your application.

4

Submit Application

Complete your application carefully and submit it according to the applicable application timeline.

Important Information

Admission deadlines, requirements and application procedures may differ depending on the program and applicant category. Students should review the information for their selected program before applying.

Preparing your application early can help you organize documents, review requirements and complete each step carefully.

Take the Next Step

Your journey at UBC begins with exploring your options and preparing your application. Discover a program that matches your interests and take the next step toward your future.
