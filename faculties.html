Faculties at UBC

The University of British Columbia offers a diverse range of academic programs across many faculties and disciplines. Students can explore their interests, develop valuable skills and prepare for meaningful careers through high-quality education and research.

Faculty of Arts

The Faculty of Arts provides students with opportunities to study humanities, social sciences, languages and creative disciplines while developing critical thinking and communication skills.

Faculty of Science

The Faculty of Science offers programs in areas such as biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics and computer science, encouraging discovery and scientific innovation.

Faculty of Applied Science

Students in Applied Science develop technical knowledge and practical skills to solve real-world problems through engineering, technology and innovation.

Sauder School of Business

The Sauder School of Business prepares students for the changing world of business through education in management, finance, marketing and entrepreneurship.

Faculty of Education

The Faculty of Education focuses on teaching, learning and educational research while preparing students to contribute to schools and communities.

Faculty of Forestry

The Faculty of Forestry explores forests, natural resources, environmental sustainability and the relationship between people and the natural world.

Faculty of Land and Food Systems

This faculty focuses on food systems, sustainable agriculture, environmental science and the relationship between land, food and society.

Peter A. Allard School of Law

The Allard School of Law provides students with a strong foundation in legal education, research, justice and professional practice.

Learning Beyond the Classroom

UBC faculties provide students with opportunities to learn through research, projects, collaboration and practical experiences. These opportunities allow students to connect academic knowledge with real-world challenges.

Through interdisciplinary learning and collaboration, students can develop the knowledge, creativity and skills needed to contribute to their communities and future professions.

Discover Your Academic Path

With a wide variety of faculties and academic programs, UBC provides students with opportunities to explore their interests, develop their skills and build a strong foundation for their future.
