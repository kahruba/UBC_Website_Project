UBC Campuses

As one of the world's top research universities, the University of British Columbia has created positive change at home and abroad for more than a century. Today our two major campuses—in Vancouver and the Okanagan—attract, nurture and transform students from Canada and around the world.

Vancouver Campus

Located near downtown Vancouver, the Vancouver campus is surrounded by beautiful natural scenery where mountains meet the ocean. It is UBC's largest campus and provides students with a vibrant academic and cultural environment.

Okanagan Campus

Located in Kelowna, British Columbia, the Okanagan campus combines a young and dynamic environment with the resources and reputation of one of Canada's leading universities.

Campus Facilities

UBC provides students with a wide range of facilities designed to support learning, research, recreation and student life.

Libraries

Access extensive collections, study spaces and digital resources to support academic success.

Research Facilities

Modern laboratories and research spaces support innovation across many different academic disciplines.

Student Centres

Dedicated spaces where students can connect, relax, participate in activities and access support services.

Sports & Recreation

Students can participate in sports, fitness programs and recreational activities throughout the year.

Student Life at UBC

Life at UBC goes beyond classrooms and lecture halls. Students have many opportunities to participate in clubs, societies, sports, cultural activities and community events.

These experiences allow students to build friendships, develop leadership skills and become active members of the university community.

Find Your Place at UBC

Whether you choose the vibrant Vancouver campus or the dynamic Okanagan campus, UBC offers an environment where students can learn, explore, connect and prepare for their future.
